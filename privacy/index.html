BeatBoxd Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 27, 2026

BeatBoxd ("we") is an app for logging and rating music. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

Data you give us

  • Email address, and sign-in details from Apple or Google if you use them
  • Display name, username, bio and profile photo (optional)
  • Your ratings, reviews, comments, lists, favorites and follows

Data collected automatically

  • A push notification token, if you allow notifications, so we can send them
  • Crash reports and performance data (through Sentry). These are not tied to your account.
  • Anonymous usage events, such as which screens are opened (through PostHog). These are not tied to your account.

We do not collect your location, contacts, or microphone or camera data. The app reads your photo library only when you choose a profile photo.

How we use it

To run the app: sign you in, show your profile and reviews to the people you choose, show the activity of people you follow, send notifications you allow, fix crashes and improve the app. We do not sell your data and we do not use it to track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Who sees it

Your profile, ratings and reviews are visible to other BeatBoxd users. Other users can follow you, comment on your reviews and report content. You can block users.

Services we use

Supabase (accounts and database), Sentry (crash reports), PostHog (anonymous usage), Apple and Google (sign-in), and music data from MusicBrainz, Deezer and Last.fm. Searching sends your search text to some of these services to return results.

Keeping and deleting your data

Delete your account any time in Settings. This permanently removes your profile, ratings, reviews, comments, follows and lists. If you signed in with Apple, we also revoke BeatBoxd's Apple sign-in access. You can also contact us using the details below.

Children

BeatBoxd is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We will post changes here and update the date above.
