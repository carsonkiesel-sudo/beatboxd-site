BeatBoxd Support

Questions, bugs, or a request to remove content or delete your data? We want to hear about it.

Delete your account

Open BeatBoxd, go to Profile, then Settings, then Delete My Account. This removes your profile, ratings, reviews, comments, follows and lists.

Report a review or a person

In the app, tap the flag on a review or comment, or open a profile and choose Report. Reports are reviewed promptly. You can also block someone from their profile.
